Which Windows can run it?

Modern websites need a modern engine. Retro Explorer uses Microsoft's Edge WebView2, so it runs wherever that engine does.

Click the star with the green arrow to pin any site under the address bar.

Privacy

Retro Explorer has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It never sends anything to us, because there is no Retro Explorer server.

Stays on your PC

  • Favorites, history and typed addresses are saved in %AppData%\RetroExplorer\settings.json.
  • Cookies, cache and site logins are saved in %AppData%\RetroExplorer\WebData.
  • Tools > Delete Browsing History clears history, typed addresses, cookies and cached files. Deleting the RetroExplorer folder in %AppData% removes everything.

Goes to other companies

  • Websites you visit receive your requests directly, the same as in any browser.
  • Bing receives your searches from the search box, and anything you type in the address bar that isn't a web address.
  • Microsoft makes the WebView2 engine that shows the pages. Microsoft Defender SmartScreen checks addresses against its list of dangerous sites, and the engine may send diagnostic data based on your Windows settings. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Questions

Is this Internet Explorer?

No. Retro Explorer is an independent project inspired by the look of browsers from around 2007–2010. It isn't made by or affiliated with Microsoft. It shows pages with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 engine.

Why can't it run on Windows XP or Vista?

Opening today's websites takes a current browser engine with modern security. The newest engine that ever ran on XP is from 2016, and nothing modern ever shipped for Vista. A retro look on top of an old engine would just show broken pages.

Is it safe to use on Windows 7?

It works, but the Edge 109 engine for Windows 7 and 8.1 stopped getting security updates in 2023. Use it for fun and nostalgia, and avoid banking or other sensitive sites on those systems.

Where are my favorites and history stored?

In %AppData%\RetroExplorer on your PC. Use Tools > Delete Browsing History to clear history, typed addresses and cookies.

How do I uninstall it?

Delete the Retro Explorer folder. To remove your saved data as well, delete %AppData%\RetroExplorer.
